# Dallara P217 LMP2 — "NOLE 24" · Novak Djokovic Career Tribute Livery

A professional Le Mans / WEC-style prototype livery concept for the **iRacing
Dallara P217 LMP2**, built as a tribute to Novak Djokovic's complete tennis
career. Designed as a clean, race-ready factory-team scheme — not a fantasy
show car.

> **What this is:** a hand-built **vector concept sheet** (5 views + colour and
> sponsor spec) intended as a reference for recreating the livery in Trading
> Paints. It is a stylised technical illustration with schematic prototype
> proportions, not a photographic 3D render. Use it as the design bible when
> painting the official Trading Paints P217 template.

**Ratio:** ~70% navy, ~18% white, ~8% red, ~4% optic-yellow + gold accents.
Navy dominates the body; white is reserved for the number panels and the
engine-cover court graphic; red is a thin energetic accent (flank band,
splitter, wing leading edges); optic-yellow only for the ball-trajectory
flow lines so it stays a highlight, never a field colour.

## Design elements

1. **Race number 24** — the hero. Large italic `24` on both doors/sidepods,
   the nose, the roof and the tail. References Djokovic's **24 Grand Slam
   titles**. Small `24 GRAND SLAMS` lockup sits under the door number.
2. **Tennis-court line graphics** — white court markings integrated into the
   **hood, roof and engine cover** (plan view shows the full court on the
   rear deck; a court band wraps the nose).
3. **Monochrome Djokovic profile** — a tasteful, low-contrast halftone profile
   silhouette **blended into both sidepods**, tinted into the navy so it reads
   only on close inspection. Deliberately **not** an oversized portrait.
4. **Serbian flag details** — the red/blue/white tricolour on the **mirrors**,
   **wing endplates** and small accent locations.
5. **Grand Slam references** — `AO · RG · W · US` title-tally text on the
   **shark fin** and rear bodywork.
6. **Four Grand Slam trophies** — clean gold cup icons grouped on the upper
   bodywork (front deck on the sides; rear deck on the plan view).
7. **24 stars** — small white stars integrated subtly across the navy panels.
8. **Tennis-ball trajectory graphics** — dynamic optic-yellow flow lines
   sweeping nose-to-tail, evoking ball flight and giving the scheme motion.

## Notes

- Visual language: modern WEC / Le Mans LMP2 prototype, premium factory-team
  appearance — clean, aggressive, aerodynamic.
- Symmetric scheme: both sides are drawn nose-left so all numbers and
  wordmarks stay readable; mirror geometry as needed when mapping to the car.
- This is an unofficial fan tribute concept — not affiliated with or endorsed
  by Novak Djokovic, the ATP, or any listed brand.
